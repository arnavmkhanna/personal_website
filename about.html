About Me.

At heart, I like to analyse and solve problems. Often, these problems involve physics, code, and/or real-world constraint (but not always!). I learn about and build things, from cancer cures to the Olympics of Robotics, and I'm drawn to complex challenges. Cambridge gives me many academic tools. I like to polish other skills myself.

Professionally, I'm still early in my career. However, my interest is piqued by problems at the frontier: new technology as well as making existing technology more accessible. I build from first principles, and my Indo-Zambian background allows me to notice things others may miss.

Outside of academics, I climbed Mt Kilimanjaro on my 18th birthday, and I’m an avid runner (currently up to half-marathon distance). I like to travel and explore, having visited 17 countries. I also read voraciously across a spectrum of genres: history, science, philosophy, finance, and mystery being some of my favourites.

Core Values

  • → Future-oriented. Whatever I do, I do to improve and build a better future.
  • → Breadth and depth. They build off each other, as in T-shaped thinking.
  • → Learning by building. Theory grows when it's forced to survive contact with real implementations.
  • → Discipline. Running has taught me that consistency > intensity.
  • → Self-Agency. Whether as a team leader, team member, or solo, I keep deadlines.
  • → Bias towards Action. I'd rather have a working prototype than a 100% perfect blueprint.
  • → Antifragility. What doesn't kill you makes you stronger.
  • → First Principles Thinking. The Why, How, and What take precedence over assumptions and past practices.
  • → Failing Forwards. Errors and missteps are data to be learnt from.
  • → Moonshot Thinking. Radical results come from audacious goals.
  • → Curiosity. The world is too large to ever stop learning, and life is the eternal teacher.
